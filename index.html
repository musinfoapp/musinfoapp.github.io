Musinfo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Musinfo is an iOS app that helps you explore your Apple Music library and listening statistics. We believe your music library and listening habits should remain private.

Music Library Access

With your permission, Musinfo accesses your device's music library to display listening statistics, favorites, music history, and weekly recaps.

This includes information such as songs, artists, albums, play counts, skip counts, and listening dates.

Data Collection and Storage

Musinfo does not collect your personal information or upload your music library or listening statistics to developer-operated servers.

Your listening statistics are calculated on your device. Certain information, including song metadata, listening recap data, and artist images, is stored locally to support app features and improve performance.

Apple Music Services

Musinfo uses Apple's MusicKit services to retrieve artist information and artwork. These requests may send artist names or music catalog identifiers to Apple.

When you choose to play music or create playlists, Apple's music services may be used. Playlists may synchronize through your Apple Music library, depending on your settings.

Apple's services are subject to Apple's own privacy practices.

Analytics and Sharing

Musinfo does not contain advertisements, third-party analytics, or tracking software.

Your listening statistics are never automatically shared with other apps. You can choose to share images of your statistics using the iOS share sheet.

Notifications

With your permission, Musinfo may schedule local notifications reminding you when your weekly listening recap is ready. These notifications are generated on your device.

Your Privacy Choices

You can revoke Musinfo's music library access or notification permissions through your device's Settings.

Revoking music library access does not automatically delete previously stored app data.

Deleting Musinfo removes its locally stored app data from your device. Previously created playlists remain in your music library, and older device backups may retain copies of some app data.

Musinfo does not require an account.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Any updates will be reflected on this page.
